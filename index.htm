01
Mom's Care Project · Brand Strategy

KAYURA HOMEMADE

"Where Ayurvedic heritage meets modern luxury."

A homemade Ayurvedic skincare brand without premium positioning or a name that carried its origin story.

The Mass Affluent consumer doesn't want cheap wellness. They want heritage with credibility — and a product that feels like it was made for them.

Named the brand 'Kayura' — a fusion of 'Kavita' (the founder's mother) and 'Ayurveda' — anchoring the brand in maternal care and ancient wisdom. Positioned against the Mass Affluent segment with luxury-grade visual identity.

A brand with a story worth telling: purple and gold visual identity, 100% organic formulations, and a positioning that bridges heritage and aspiration.

Tech-Hustle Project · GTM Strategy
"Closing the trust gap in India's gig economy."

Gig workers in India's events sector couldn't trust that they'd be paid on time — or at all.

Trust is not built through branding. It's built through systems. The product itself had to be the promise.

GTM focused on the Events Sector as a high-frequency, high-stakes entry point. Positioned WorkPluss around Instant Payout as the brand's core proof point, with a lean 10% commission model that signalled worker-first values.

A platform brand built on reliability, not aesthetics — launching 2025 via Dhruvanta Tech Pvt. Ltd.
