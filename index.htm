Mom's Care Project / Luxury Skincare Strategy

Kayura Homemade

Challenge

To scale an intimate homemade venture inspired by maternal care into a high-resonance, luxury Ayurvedic brand targeted directly at India's fast-growing "Mass Affluent" consumers who seek chemical-free, premium lifestyle alternatives.

My Role
  • Brand Positioning
  • Naming Logic
  • Color Psychology
  • Consumer Persona
  • Brand Values
  • Visual Identity
  • Founder Vision

Developed a premium positioning framework and naming architecture that bridged deep Ayurvedic tradition with modern expectations. The name Kayura honors the founder's mother (Kavita) while signaling safety, pure lineage, and heritage confidence.

Outcome

Created a premium Ayurvedic positioning that balanced heritage with modern luxury. Established a sensory design direction of regal warmth—using deep purple and striking white—to honor native Indian botanicals while maintaining premium shelf readiness.

Tech-Hustle Architecture / Growth Framework

WorkPluss (Dhruvanta Tech)

Challenge

The student gig economy faces a massive employment gap across Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian ecosystems, fundamentally blocked by payment anxiety. This creates a systemic trust deficit between platforms, students, and corporate partners.

My Role
  • Brand Strategy
  • GTM Thinking
  • Consumer Positioning
  • Messaging
  • Business Model Communication
  • Presentation Design

Reframed structural platform features into a compelling "Instant Payout Advantage" narrative. Designed a strategic presentation and messaging framework that clearly communicated trust, security, and systemic innovation to both end-users and investors.

Outcome

Delivered a high-clarity strategic communication architecture that simplified complex marketplace mechanics. This lean monetization narrative enabled rapid student brand ambassadorship and seamless corporate pipeline onboarding.
