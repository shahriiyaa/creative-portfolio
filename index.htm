About Riya

I'm Riya Shah, a strategist and founder drawn to the emotional side of business: the stories people carry, the identities they aspire toward, and the quiet psychology behind what resonates.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy, culture, consumer behavior, and storytelling.

From building a heritage-led skincare brand to shaping trust-first systems for the gig economy, I'm interested in ideas that combine clarity with emotional depth. I'm especially drawn to brands that feel human — thoughtful, intentional, and culturally aware.
