Mom's Care Project / Luxury Skincare Strategy

Kayura Homemade

The Strategic Opportunity

To scale an intimate homemade venture inspired by maternal care into a high-resonance, luxury Ayurvedic brand targeted directly at India's fast-growing "Mass Affluent" consumers who seek chemical-free, premium lifestyle alternatives.

The Story Behind the Naming Architecture

The name Kayura carries deep personal meaning. It bridges deep Ayurvedic tradition directly with my mother's name, Kavita. It builds an immediate positioning matrix around safety, pure lineage, and absolute heritage confidence.

Visual Identity Realization

Utilizing a sensory design direction of regal warmth using deep purple (#6A1C57) and vibrant orange (#F89B1F) to honor native Indian botanicals while maintaining premium shelf value.

Tech-Hustle Architecture / Growth Framework

WorkPluss (Dhruvanta)

The Strategic Opportunity

Addressing the massive employment gap for college students across Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian ecosystems by providing access to flexible part-time assignments within corporate on-ground configurations, production networks, and event structures.

Systemic Trust Deficit Resolution

The core friction point in the student gig economy is payment anxiety. WorkPluss solves this structural deficit completely through the "Instant Payout Advantage" framework—ensuring immediate security post-assignment verification.

Go-To-Market Mechanics

Deploying a lean 10% marketplace monetization match model, focusing rollouts directly through events management associations, university club nodes, and rapid student brand ambassador pipelines.
